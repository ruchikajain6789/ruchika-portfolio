CareerMatch AI

Overview

CareerMatch AI is an AI-powered job matching platform designed to help job seekers discover relevant opportunities and evaluate how well their resumes align with job requirements. It uses semantic similarity to compare resume content with job descriptions and help users identify relevant opportunities more effectively.

My Contribution

  • ✓ Implemented resume upload and text extraction for PDF and DOCX files
  • ✓ Integrated AI-based semantic similarity to compare resumes with job descriptions
  • ✓ Built job-saving and application-tracking features
  • ✓ Developed resume analysis to help users understand their job relevance
